IS 13252 (Part 1) : 2010
IEC 60950-1 : 2005
INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY EQUIPMENT - SAFETY
PART 1 GENERAL REQUIREMENTS

1.1 Scope
1.1.1 Equipment covered by this standard
This standard is applicable to mains-powered or battery-powered information technology equipment, including electrical business equipment and associated equipment, with a RATED VOLTAGE not exceeding 600 V.

1.2 Definitions
1.2.4.1 CLASS I EQUIPMENT
equipment where protection against electric shock is achieved by using BASIC INSULATION and providing a means of connection to the PROTECTIVE EARTHING CONDUCTOR.

1.5 Components
1.5.1 General
Where safety is involved, components shall comply either with the requirements of this standard or with the safety aspects of the relevant IEC component standards.

2.1 Protection from electric shock and energy hazards
2.1.1 Protection in operator access areas
This subclause specifies requirements for protection against electric shock from energized parts.
